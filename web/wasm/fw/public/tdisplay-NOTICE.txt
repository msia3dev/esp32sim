Licences of the T-Display spot-price demo (tdisplay-prices.json)
================================================================

Files: tdisplay-vfs.bin (this directory) — a LittleFS image of examples/ttgo-tdisplay, built by
examples/ttgo-tdisplay/build-vfs.sh; and ESP32_GENERIC-20260824-v1.29.0.bin (web/wasm/fw/, not
committed: fetched from micropython.org by tools/fetch-micropython.sh, SHA-256
e67ad6015a0a504c1fec9aa9bbf589d0432ed28e62546f4f8dd8a147f8bd95f6), the official MicroPython
v1.29.0 image for the generic ESP32 board, which the Pages site serves next to this file.

tdisplay-vfs.bin contains
-------------------------
- app.mpy, main.py, config.json, demo_prices.json: this repository's demo (MIT, LICENSE at the
  root); demo_prices.json is one day of spot prices in the format of elprisetjustnu.se's API.
- st7789py.mpy: russhughes/st7789py_mpy (st7789py.py), MIT, Copyright (c) 2020-2023 Russ Hughes,
  Copyright (c) 2019 Ivan Belokobylskiy (based on devbis/st7789py_mpy).
- vga1_8x16.mpy, vga2_16x32.mpy: the bitmap fonts of russhughes/st7789py_mpy (fonts/bitmap,
  converted from the classic PC BIOS text-mode fonts), MIT, same copyright.
The sources are in examples/ttgo-tdisplay with their notices.
